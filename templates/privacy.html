Privacy overview

Last updated {{ current_year }}. This page is a high-level summary — have counsel review before an IPO or regulated launch.

What we process

Account details you provide at registration (club name, email, hashed password, Play-Cricket base URL). Relay operations fetch public ECB Play-Cricket HTML required to derive scores for overlays you configure.

Where data lives

Deployment is under your control (for example AWS EC2 + RDS in the UK). Configure region pinning, encryption at rest, and backups to meet your league obligations.

Cookies & analytics

The web product uses session cookies for login. Add your own marketing analytics and consent banners when you enable tags on the marketing site.

CricRelay Live mobile app

The CricRelay Stream Android app (CricRelay Live) may collect:

  • Crash and diagnostic data via Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to fix stability issues. Stack traces may include device model and OS version; we do not intentionally log stream keys or passwords.
  • Anonymous usage events via Firebase Analytics (Google), such as sign-in success, stream creation, and go-live outcomes. Events do not include your email, stream keys, or RTMP URLs.
  • Camera and microphone while you broadcast — audio/video is sent only to the RTMP destination you configure (for example YouTube or Twitch), not to CricRelay servers for storage.
  • Account email when you sign in to your club server.
  • RTMP stream keys stored on your device for volunteer streaming (see app Data safety in Google Play).

You can disable analytics collection in Android settings for Google Play services where available. Crash reporting helps us improve reliability for club volunteers.

Contact

For privacy requests, reach the data controller email you publish for your deployment.
