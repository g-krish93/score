Privacy overview

Last updated {{ current_year }}. This page is a high-level summary — have counsel review before an IPO or regulated launch.

What we process

Account details you provide at registration (club name, email, hashed password, Play-Cricket base URL). Relay operations fetch public ECB Play-Cricket HTML required to derive scores for overlays you configure.

Where data lives

Deployment is under your control (for example AWS EC2 + RDS in the UK). Configure region pinning, encryption at rest, and backups to meet your league obligations.

Cookies & analytics

The product uses session cookies for login. Add your own analytics & consent banners when you enable marketing tags.

Contact

For privacy requests, reach the data controller email you publish for your deployment.
