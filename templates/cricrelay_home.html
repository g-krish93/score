FAQ

Do viewers need to install anything?

No — overlays render as browser sources inside Prism, OBS, or mobile broadcast stacks.

Does CricRelay replace Play-Cricket scoring?

No — clubs continue scoring in ECB tools. CricRelay relays that truth into overlays.

Can we show only our own club page publicly?

Yes. Each club has its own public page and only that club's data appears there.
